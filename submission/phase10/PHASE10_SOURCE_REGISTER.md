### 2.3 Venue 3: IEEE/ACM Transactions on Networking (ToN)

- **Official Journal Portal:** `https://www.comsoc.org/publications/journals/ieee-tnet`
- **Author Guidelines & Policies:** `https://www.comsoc.org/publications/journals/ieee-tnet/policies-guidelines`
- **Access Date:** 12 November 2026
- **Submission Model:** Rolling submissions via ScholarOne Manuscripts (`https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/ton-ieee`).
- **Official Scope:**
  - *Quotation:* "The IEEE/ACM Transactions on Networking (ToN) publishes original theoretical and practical research contributions in the area of communication networks... topics include network architecture and design, network protocols, network software, routing, traffic control, network measurement, wireless and mobile networks, and network operations."
- **Review Model & Formatting:**
  - *Review Model:* **Single-anonymous (single-blind)**. Reviewers know author identities and affiliations; submissions must include complete author metadata and affiliations on the title page.
  - *Template:* IEEE Transactions two-column, 10-point font template.
  - *Length Allowance:* Maximum 16 pages typically for initial submission. Accepted papers receive **10 pages free of charge**.
  - *Mandatory Overlength Page Charges:* Pages exceeding 10 pages in the final published paper incur a mandatory charge of **$220 per excess page**.
  - *Open Access Policy:* Hybrid journal. Standard publication in IEEE Xplore under subscription access is **$0 author fee** (no mandatory Article Processing Charge if within 10 pages). Optional Open Access incurs the standard IEEE hybrid APC ($2,495).
  - *Author Requirements:* Mandatory verified **ORCID iD** for all authors at submission.
  - *Generative AI Policy (IEEE):* Use of generative AI for text, code, or figures must be explicitly disclosed in the Acknowledgments section. AI cannot be credited as an author.

### 2.4 Cross-Venue Publication Ethics Policies

- **ACM Policy on Authorship and Peer Review:** `https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/authorship-policy`
  - All authors must be human individuals who made significant intellectual contributions.
  - Generative AI tools cannot be listed as authors.
  - AI used in the conduct of research (data analysis, model generation, simulation code) must be disclosed in the Methodology section.
- **IEEE Policy on Authorship and Generative AI:** `https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/`
  - Explicit disclosure in Acknowledgments detailing tool name, scope of use, and sections affected.
- **Simultaneous Submission Policy:**
  - ACM and IEEE strictly prohibit simultaneous submission of the same or substantially overlapping manuscripts to multiple venues. Submitting to ICPE, SoCC, or ToN simultaneously constitutes a severe ethics violation and results in immediate rejection and multi-year sanctions.
