## 3. Methodological Principles Enforced in Phase 5.3

1. **Non-Destructive Versioning:**
   Historical reports provide an accurate record of research evolution. Rather than rewriting historical documents to retroactively match later insights, all corrections are recorded in versioned qualification addenda.
2. **Explicit Qualification Over Unqualified Affirmation:**
   Claims that hold under specific assumptions (such as unconstrained non-dominance vs. operational SLA compliance, or logical primitive size vs. retained heap footprint) are categorized as **Resolved with Qualification**, clearly delineating their operational boundaries.
3. **Automated Verification:**
   All corrected mathematical relationships and pairwise dominance assertions are verified by automated unit tests in [`Phase53FinalVerificationTest.java`](file:///Users/cruise/Desktop/DREB-RateLimiter/src/test/java/com/dreb/ratelimit/Phase53FinalVerificationTest.java), ensuring reproducibility.
